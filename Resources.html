Resources & Learning More

Learning about disabilities, accessibility, and inclusion can help create a more understanding and welcoming community. The websites below provide helpful information about disability rights, support services, and opportunities for people with disabilities.

Disability Support Organizations

  • LADD, Inc. - Provides housing, employment, community programs, and support for adults with developmental disabilities.
  • Opportunities for Ohioans with Disabilities (OOD) - Provides services that help people with disabilities prepare for work, find jobs, and achieve independence.

Disability Rights

  • Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) - Learn about disability rights, accessibility, and protection against discrimination.
  • Disability Rights Ohio - Provides information and legal advocacy for people with disabilities in Ohio.

Why These Resources Matter

These organizations help people with disabilities learn about their rights, find support, and discover opportunities in their communities. Knowing where to find information can help people become more independent and included in everyday life.

Keep Learning

Disability awareness starts with understanding, respect, and inclusion. Everyone deserves the opportunity to learn, work, participate, and feel valued in their community. Remember, a disability is only one part of a person. We are all more than a label.
