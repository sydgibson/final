Disablity Awareness

Disablity awareness is about understanding disablities, promoting accessibility, inclusion and breaking sterotypes. People with disablities are more then their disablity

Welcome

Welcome to More then a Label. This website is a place to learn about disablites, accessibility, and inclusion. My goal is to help people understand disablity and see the person beyond the label. Throughout this website you will learn about different types of disablites and the challenges people with disablites may face. You will also learn about the importance about accessibility and how small changes can help create a more inclusive environment. The website also focuses on breaking sterotypes and showing that people with disablites have their own talent, goals, intrest and abilities. Disablity does not define a person and everyone deserves to be treated with respect, understanding, and equality.
