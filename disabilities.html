Types of Disabilities

There are many diffrent types of disabilities. They can affect a person's movement, communication, learning, senses or daily activities.

  • Physical Disabilities- can affect a person's movemen, strength, or ablity to perform certin physical activities.
  • Sensory Disabilities- can affect a person's ability to see or hear
  • Intellectual and Developmental Disabilities- can affect learninhg, communication, problem-solving and everyday skills.
  • Invisible Disabilities- are disabilities that may not be immediately noticeable to other people.

Eveyone's Experience is Different

Everyone experiences disability differently. People may have have diffrent abilities, needs, challanges,and goals. It is imprtant not to make assumptions about someone based off their disability because every person has their own story to tell.
